Frequently Asked Questions

What is medical claims auditing?

Medical claims auditing is a systematic review of healthcare bills and insurance claims to verify accuracy, identify billing errors, and ensure you are not being overcharged. An auditor reviews your Explanation of Benefits (EOB), medical bills, and insurance payments to spot discrepancies such as duplicate charges, upcoding, or services billed but not rendered.

How can a medical claims audit save me money?

Medical billing errors are extremely common — studies show that up to 80% of medical bills contain mistakes. A claims audit can identify overcharges, duplicate billing, incorrect procedure codes, and services billed but not provided. Many patients recover hundreds or even thousands of dollars in out-of-pocket costs through a thorough audit.

What types of claims do you review?

We review all types of medical claims including hospital bills, physician charges, surgical claims, outpatient services, diagnostic tests, lab work, and prescription claims. We work with both commercial insurance policies and Medicare/Medicaid claims for patients in Marietta, GA and beyond.

How long does a medical claims audit take?

The timeline depends on the complexity and volume of claims. A single claim review typically takes 3–5 business days. More complex cases involving multiple claims or appeal processes may take 2–4 weeks. We keep you informed throughout the entire process and work efficiently to resolve issues as quickly as possible.

Is my medical information kept confidential?

Absolutely. COA Auditing is fully HIPAA-compliant and follows strict data security protocols. Your medical information is never shared with third parties and is used solely for the purpose of conducting your claims audit. Confidentiality and security are our top priorities.
